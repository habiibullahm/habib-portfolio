what: "A podcast tracker and learning journal: find real episodes via iTunes search (or paste a YouTube link), listen, capture timestamped notes and highlights, get an AI summary grounded in what was actually said, and export everything to Obsidian or Notion."
why: "Podcast notes usually live nowhere, or get typed from memory hours later. PodMark lets you take notes with timestamps while you listen, and its AI summary is based on a transcript of what was actually said, not on a guess from the title. If an episode has no transcript or show notes, the summary cannot be based on what was said, so PodMark falls back to a cautious overview from the title."
how: "React/Vite frontend with Zustand state, deployed as a single Vercel project (static frontend + /api/* serverless functions, no CORS). Audio is transcribed via Groq Whisper (segment-level timestamps, per-error-code handling for rate limits, quota, and oversized files); the summary step runs through SumoPod's OpenAI-compatible gateway, grounding on the transcript first and show notes second; only when neither exists does it fall back to a hedged general-knowledge overview from the title rather than refusing to answer. Accounts are optional — signed-out users get local-only storage — and the AI summary endpoint requires a verified Supabase session specifically to protect API credits from public abuse."
