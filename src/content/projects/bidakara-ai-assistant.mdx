what: "An independent prototype that answers questions about Bidakara Medical Center from its official public website: clinic addresses, services, doctors, and practice schedules. General answers quote and cite the official page they come from; doctor and schedule answers are read from structured tables. It is not a medical service, a registration system, or an official Bidakara product."
why: "A clinic assistant that guesses is worse than none: a wrong schedule sends someone to a closed clinic, and a confident answer to a health question is unsafe. The goal was answers people can verify, and a clear hand-off to official channels when the sources don't support an answer."
how: "A Next.js app over a PostgreSQL + pgvector index built from the official website. General answers must be exact quotes from a retrieved official page; doctor and schedule answers come from structured tables."
flow:
  - "Ingest: a CLI fetches only allowlisted HTTPS pages on the official domain, renders the branch pages that load doctors client-side in headless Chromium, and skips unchanged content by hash. A failed fetch or embedding run leaves the previous index intact."
  - "Filter: health, emergency, and personal-data questions are caught before retrieval and never stored."
  - "Retrieve: cosine similarity, keyword search, and metadata filters over documents, chunks, and clinic, doctor, and schedule tables (1536-dimension embeddings)."
  - "Answer: an exact quote with its source link. Stale or conditional schedules (On Call, by appointment) are not shown as fixed hours, and anything unsupported falls back to the official channels."
decision: "Official pages disagree on BiMedCare+ registration timing, and in one staging diagnosis the model answered anyway. A deterministic conflict guard now runs before retrieval and evidence selection and returns a notice instead, so the model cannot pick a side."
evaluation: "Latest online run (52 cases, 28 Sep 2026): 40/40 retrieval hits, 36/40 expected factual answers, 5/5 correct no-answers, p95 latency 2.6 s. Three misses were abstentions and one cited a related page instead of the exact one. This is an automated check, not human or clinical review. CI runs typecheck, lint, unit tests, a 52-case offline guardrail evaluation, and Playwright E2E against real PostgreSQL with only the model provider faked."
