what: "An independent prototype that answers questions about Bidakara Medical Center from its official public website: clinic addresses, services, doctors, and practice schedules. General answers quote and cite the official page they come from; doctor and schedule answers are read from structured tables. It is not a medical service, a registration system, or an official Bidakara product."
why: "A clinic assistant that guesses is worse than none: a wrong schedule sends someone to a closed clinic, and a confident answer to a health question is unsafe. The goal was answers people can verify, and a clear hand-off to official channels when the sources don't support an answer."
how: "Ingestion is a CLI that fetches only allowlisted HTTPS pages on the official domain, renders the branch pages that load doctors client-side in headless Chromium, and is idempotent by content hash: a failed fetch or embedding run leaves the previous index intact. Documents, chunks, 1536-dimension embeddings, and clinic/doctor/schedule tables live in PostgreSQL with pgvector. Retrieval combines cosine similarity, keyword search, and metadata filters. Health, emergency, and personal-data questions are filtered before retrieval and never stored. General answers must be exact quotes from a retrieved official chunk, and schedules that are stale or conditional (On Call, by appointment) are not presented as fixed hours. One decision came from a staging failure: official pages disagree on BiMedCare+ registration timing, and in one staging diagnosis the model answered anyway. A deterministic conflict guard now runs before retrieval and evidence selection and returns a notice instead, so the model cannot pick a side. Latest online evaluation (52 cases, 28 Sep 2026): 40/40 retrieval hits, 36/40 expected factual answers, 5/5 correct no-answers, p95 latency 2.6 s. Three misses were abstentions and one cited a related page instead of the exact one. That run is an automated check, not human or clinical review. CI runs typecheck, lint, unit tests, a 52-case offline guardrail evaluation, and Playwright E2E against real PostgreSQL with only the model provider faked."
